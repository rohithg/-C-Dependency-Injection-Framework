# Energy analytics metric glossary

| Metric | Definition | Owner | Grain |
|--|--|--|--|
| Total kWh | Sum of metered / attributed energy savings or usage in scope | Program Ops | utility × program × day |
| Peak Demand kW | Max coincident peak in period | Grid Ops | utility × program × day |
| Incentive Spend | Incentives paid to participants | Finance | utility × program × day |
| Compliance Rate | Share of rows flagged compliant for regulatory filing | Compliance | utility × program × day |
| Cost per kWh Saved | Incentive Spend ÷ Total kWh | Finance | same |

Synthetic demo data — method mirrors utility program reporting.
