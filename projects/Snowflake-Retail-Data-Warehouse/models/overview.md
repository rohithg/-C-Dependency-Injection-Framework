# Snowflake Retail Data Warehouse

ELT warehouse for retail and supply-chain analytics. Raw ERP (NetSuite), CRM,
TMS, and YMS data is staged, joined in intermediate models, and published as a
star schema (`dim_*` / `fct_*`) for BI.

Maintained by [Rohith Gangapuram](https://github.com/rohithg).
