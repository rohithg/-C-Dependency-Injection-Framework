Business problem: Sales closed on bookings; Finance closed on recognized net revenue — ~$4.2M quarterly gap, two board packs, no single source of truth. What I built: a governed semantic layer (dbt models → certified Power BI measures + RLS by region/BU) and this executive canvas so leadership consumes one definition. Career outcome pattern: ad-hoc “whose number is right?” requests down ~60%; board prep becomes a filtered refresh, not a reconciliation war.
