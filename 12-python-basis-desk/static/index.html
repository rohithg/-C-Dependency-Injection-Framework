Basis / read the bet

Paste a Polymarket link. Basis pulls the live market and tells you what the price already says, what a $100 bet pays, and when to get out. It does not place the bet and it does not invent news.

The busiest open markets load on the left. Pick one, or paste a link.

Paper reading only. Not a broker and not financial advice. The price is the forecast of everyone already in the market. Basis has no information they lack. Notes stay in this browser.
